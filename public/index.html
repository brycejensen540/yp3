How it works

Three steps, no clutter.

yp3 is a thin, honest wrapper around two trusted open-source tools: yt-dlp for fetching and ffmpeg for encoding. Nothing is re-uploaded to a third party.

  1. 01

    Paste a link

    Drop in any video or track URL. yp3 recognises the source and reads its details before downloading.

  2. 02

    We fetch the audio

    The best audio-only stream the site offers is downloaded — never a low-bitrate screen capture.

  3. 03

    Download your MP3

    ffmpeg encodes a constant 320 kbps file, tags it with the title and artist, and hands it over.

About the audio

Maximum quality, stated plainly.

MP3 is a lossy format. No encoder can make an MP3 file lossless, and any site claiming otherwise is stretching the truth. What yp3 does is squeeze out everything the format allows: a constant 320 kbps encode from the highest-quality source stream, with no extra re-encoding along the way.

If the source itself is a low-bitrate upload, the result will match that source. You get an exact ceiling, not a promise you can't hear.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Still stuck? Open an issue on the GitHub repository.

Is the MP3 lossless?

No — MP3 is a lossy format by design, so a "lossless MP3" does not exist. yp3 encodes at 320 kbps constant bitrate, the highest quality MP3 supports, from the best audio stream available. That is the practical ceiling for an MP3.

Which sites can I use?

YouTube, Vimeo, SoundCloud, TikTok, X, Facebook, Instagram, Dailymotion, Bandcamp, Twitch, Reddit, Archive.org and other public sites supported by yt-dlp. Links to private, members-only or age-restricted content cannot be converted.

How long does a conversion take?

Usually well under a minute for a typical song. Longer videos scale with length, since the audio stream is downloaded and then encoded. The progress bar tracks both stages.

What happens to my files?

Finished MP3s are stored in a temporary directory and deleted automatically after 45 minutes, or as soon as the server restarts. Nothing is kept for analytics, and no link to your download is shared.

Why did my link fail?

Common causes: the video is private or removed, it is live right now, it is region locked, or the source site is temporarily limiting automated requests. Retrying in a few minutes usually clears the last case.

Can I use this for anything?

Only download content you own or have permission to use. Respect the terms of service of the source site and the rights of the creator. yp3 is a tool, not a licence.
